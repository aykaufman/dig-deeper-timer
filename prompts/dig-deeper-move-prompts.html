Dig Deeper Move Prompts

Image prompts for all 124 moves in the program. Tap Copy and paste into ChatGPT, Gemini or any image generator.

How to get the same man in every picture
  1. Open one chat and paste the first prompt.
  2. Keep pasting the next prompts in that same chat. It will usually keep the same man and gym.
  3. If he starts to change, upload your best image and add “Use the same man and gym as this photo.”
  4. Check every picture before you trust it. If the back rounds, elbows flare, or a dumbbell looks wrong, ask it to fix that one thing.
